# Quiet Impossibility — House Style v1

## Core idea

A familiar, materially convincing object or scene contains one conceptual
anomaly. The anomaly may be an impossible physical behavior, an impossible
material transition, an imbalance in ordinary physics, or a familiar object
made from an unexpectedly fragile or contradictory material.

Present it calmly and without explanation. It should feel discovered rather
than performed: poetic, tactile, restrained, and legible within one or two
seconds.

## Canonical evidence

### Anchor 1 — stone and linen

`../assets/anchor-stone-linen.png`

A moss-covered river stone rests on cream linen while the fabric forms
concentric ripples as though it were water. This establishes tactile realism,
the physical treatment of the anomaly, imperfect organic texture, and the
quietness of the result.

### Anchor 2 — glass knife switch

`../assets/anchor-glass-switch.png`

A minimal knife switch uses a thick bottle-green glass lever on an aged cream
ceramic base. This establishes object simplicity, unlikely material
substitution, restrained bottle-green and brass accents, patina, and generous
negative space.

### Validated stress test — linen light

`../assets/stress-test-linen-light-door.png`

A freestanding cream door emits light that becomes folded linen on the floor.
This validates architectural scale and slightly more narrative scenes. It also
marks a boundary: luminosity is acceptable when it remains materially embodied
and does not become fantasy spectacle.

## Visual DNA

- **Medium:** Photorealistic conceptual editorial photography. The scene should
  resemble a handmade real-world set, never a glossy render.
- **Mood:** Contemplative, enigmatic, intimate, and slightly uncanny; quiet
  rather than theatrical.
- **Palette:** Deep petrol, blue-green, charcoal, aged cream, moss, and bottle
  green. Aged brass or restrained warm light may appear as small accents.
- **Lighting:** Directional side light, restrained chiaroscuro, deep soft
  shadows, controlled highlights, and atmospheric falloff.
- **Materials:** Tactile, imperfect, visibly real surfaces such as weathered
  wood, coarse linen, worn stone, aged ceramic, thick glass, oxidized brass,
  fibrous paper, and matte painted surfaces.
- **Texture:** Muted saturation, fine photographic grain, subtle wear,
  irregular fibers, mineral texture, patina, tiny bubbles, and handmade
  imperfections.
- **Composition:** One dominant subject or interaction, a simple silhouette,
  asymmetrical balance, and generous uncluttered negative space. Editorial,
  not sterile catalog photography.
- **Anomaly:** Exactly one conceptual anomaly, physically integrated with the
  ordinary scene. Everything else behaves normally.

## Strong anomaly mechanisms

Prefer anomalies that can be shown as a physical fact:

- one material behaving like another;
- one continuous object transitioning between two materials;
- a small object carrying impossible weight or influence;
- a functional object remade in an unlikely material;
- light, shadow, fabric, paper, glass, stone, or another ordinary substance
  taking on one contradictory property.

Avoid generic floating objects. Levitation is rarely specific enough to carry
the concept on its own.

## The anomaly test

Before generation, ask:

1. Can the concept be stated in one short sentence?
2. Is the anomalous event visible without explanation?
3. If the anomaly were removed, would the image look like a plausible
   photograph?
4. Is the effect physical and tactile rather than symbolic or decorative?
5. Is there only one surreal idea?

If any answer is no, simplify the concept.

## Avoid

- glossy CGI or polished luxury advertising;
- fantasy portals, overt magic, decorative glow, or dramatic light beams;
- neon, cyberpunk, maximalism, or excessive saturation;
- clutter, ornate mechanisms, elaborate machines, or multiple surreal events;
- visual riddles, cute whimsy, melodrama, or sterile perfection;
- generic levitation or morphing without a clear conceptual relationship;
- borrowed subjects, compositions, or anomaly mechanisms from the anchors;
- text, logos, watermarks, frames, borders, or decorative rails unless the
  user explicitly requires them.

## Directioning template

Use this when the user wants concepts before generation:

```text
Using the Quiet Impossibility House Style, propose three visually distinct
concepts for this brief:

[CREATIVE BRIEF]

Each concept must contain one familiar subject and exactly one quiet physical
or material anomaly. It must be explainable in one sentence, use a different
visual mechanism from the other concepts, read immediately, and avoid
repeating the canonical anchors.

For each concept provide:
A. a short title;
B. the one-sentence image concept;
C. why it fits the brief;
D. the main visual risk.
```

## Production prompt template

Adapt only the fields relevant to the request:

```text
Asset type: [DESTINATION OR USE]
Primary request: Create a new image in the Quiet Impossibility House Style.
[ORDINARY SUBJECT AND SCENE]. Exactly one conceptual anomaly occurs:
[ANOMALY IN ONE CLEAR SENTENCE].
Input images: The packaged canonical anchors are style references only. Match
their palette, material tactility, restrained lighting, grain, calm surrealism,
and negative space. Do not reuse their subjects or compositions.
Scene/backdrop: [SIMPLE, MATERIALLY BELIEVABLE ENVIRONMENT].
Subject: [ONE DOMINANT SUBJECT OR INTERACTION].
Style/medium: Photorealistic conceptual editorial photography; handmade
real-world set; tactile and imperfect; quiet, not theatrical.
Composition/framing: [ORIENTATION AND VIEWPOINT], one clear focal interaction,
asymmetrical balance, generous uncluttered negative space.
Lighting/mood: Restrained directional side light, deep soft shadows, controlled
highlights; contemplative and slightly uncanny.
Color palette: Deep petrol, blue-green, charcoal, aged cream, moss or bottle
green; aged brass or warm light only as restrained accents.
Materials/textures: [RELEVANT MATERIALS], with visible grain, fibers, patina,
wear, and fine photographic texture.
Constraints: Exactly one anomaly. It must read immediately, feel physically
integrated, and be treated as ordinary. Everything else follows normal physics.
Avoid: glossy CGI, fantasy styling, decorative magic, unexplained glow,
elaborate machinery, clutter, multiple surreal events, generic levitation,
excessive saturation, sterile perfection, text, logos, watermarks, frames, and
borders.
```

For Notion covers, add:

```text
Composition/framing: Panoramic responsive banner. Keep the complete conceptual
interaction inside the central 60% of the canvas horizontally and vertically.
Leave expendable dark texture above, below, and at both sides. No important
detail near an edge. No text; the page title appears below the cover.
```

## Final quality gate

Accept only if the image:

1. reads as a real photograph before it reads as surreal;
2. communicates one clear anomaly at first glance;
3. feels calm, tactile, and materially specific;
4. retains substantial negative space;
5. belongs beside both canonical anchors without imitating either one;
6. preserves the intended meaning after destination-specific cropping.
